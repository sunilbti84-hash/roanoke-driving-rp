Features

Everything a Roanoke driving server needs

Six modules tuned for Star City roleplay — from the Salem Avenue strip to the Blue Ridge Parkway overlooks.

🚔

Traffic Stop Animations

Scripted pull-over sequences with officer approach, window roll-down, license handoff, and citation props — synced for every player nearby.

🪪

Authentic VA Plates

Virginia-format plate generation with Roanoke County and City prefixes, plus optional personalized plate support for civilian vehicles.

📍

Route Markers

Live map markers along US-221, US-460, and I-581 with turn-by-turn blips for delivery, taxi, and bus roleplay routes.

📡

Speed Trap Zones

Configurable radar zones with automatic speed logging, dispatch alerts, and fine calculation based on Virginia traffic code tiers.

🧰

Civilian Job Spawns

Pre-placed spawn points for tow trucks, delivery vans, taxi fleets, and bus routes across Roanoke, Salem, and Vinton.

⚙️

One-File Config

Every zone, plate prefix, and job spawn lives in a single readable config — no SQL required, no framework lock-in.
